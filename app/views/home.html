This service is replacing Get Information About Schools (GIAS)

You can continue to use GIAS until 26 July 2027 at getinformationaboutschools.service.gov.uk.

If you're looking for detailed information about individual schools, use School Profiles. The service provides more detailed school information and lets you compare schools.

Why is GIAS being replaced?

This new service focuses on providing an accurate and authoritative register of education providers, provider groups and governors in England. It has been designed to improve data quality, respond more easily to changes in data and policy requirements, and provide better access to education provider data.

People looking for detailed information about individual schools can use School Profiles, which has been designed specifically to meet those needs.
